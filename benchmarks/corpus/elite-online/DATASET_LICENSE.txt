This dataset is derived from the Lichess standard rated games database published at https://database.lichess.org/ , which Lichess releases under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). Attribution is not legally required; provenance is preserved here for transparency.
Ratings in this dataset are Lichess Glicko-2 ratings, not FIDE Elo, and must never be pooled or compared with the masters-otb collection.
